Our Story

A quiet devotion to craft.

Each rug is thoughtfully designed in our West Palm Beach studio and brought to life by master artisans all over the world who have spent generations refining their craft. We work in small editions, with natural fibers, and with the patience that hand-knotting demands.

What arrives at your door is not a product. It is the quiet result of hundreds of hours, the hands of master artisans, and a story we hope becomes part of yours.

Craftsmanship

Made to order,
made to last.

Every Four Muses rug is begun only after it has been claimed. Nothing sits in a warehouse. Nothing is mass produced. Nothing leaves our partner workshops without passing through four pairs of careful hands.

From the first dye bath to the final wash, we honor the slowness that gives a rug its soul.

01

Consult

We help you choose the design, palette, and dimensions that suit your space.

02

Source

Wool, silk, and natural dyes are gathered from trusted, traceable sources.

03

Weave

Master artisans knot every rug by hand — a process measured in weeks, not minutes.

04

Deliver

Your finished rug is carefully packed and shipped white-glove to your door.
