About Four Muses

A quiet devotion
to craft.

Four Muses is a West Palm Beach rug house. Our rugs are hand-knotted by master artisans in India, Nepal, Afghanistan and Turkey, in wool, silk and viscose, and finished by hand.

Every design in the collection can be made to order, and we keep select pieces in stock for projects on a tight schedule. Need a size or palette we don't carry? We design custom rugs for trade clients too.
